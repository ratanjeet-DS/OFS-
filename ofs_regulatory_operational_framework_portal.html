Statutory Foundations & Structural Dimensions

The Offer for Sale (OFS) mechanism provides an exchange-based transparent pathway for company promoters and non-promoter significant shareholders to dilute equity holdings. This section outlines the core regulatory thresholds established under SEBI's 2023 Master Circular, BSE Notice No. 20230127-14, and NSE Circular No. 98/2023, along with required reservation tranches and single bidder concentration caps.

Minimum Aggregate Offer
₹25 Crore

Permitted below ₹25 Cr only if promoter dilution achieves MPS compliance in a single tranche.

Retail Reservation
Min 10%

Mandatory minimum reservation allocated for retail individual investors (bids ≤ ₹2,00,000).

MF & Insurance Reservation
Min 25%

Cumulative minimum reservation reserved for SEBI-registered Mutual Funds and IRDAI Insurers.

Single Bidder Cap
Max 25%

Maximum allocation limit to any single bidder (Exempted: Mutual Funds and Insurance Companies).

Allocation Simulator: Price Priority vs. Proportionate

Assume Available Retail Quantity = 1,000 shares. Total Demand = 1,500 shares (Cut-off = ₹110).

Bidder Bid Price & Qty Price Priority (At Bid) Price Priority (At Cut-off) Proportionate Basis
Retail A 400 @ ₹110 (Cut-off) 0 shares (Exhausted) 0 shares (Exhausted) 267 shares (@ ₹110)
Retail B 500 @ ₹111 (Mid-Tier) 400 shares (@ ₹111) 400 shares (@ ₹110) 333 shares (@ ₹110)
Retail C 600 @ ₹112 (Top-Tier) 600 shares (@ ₹112) 600 shares (@ ₹110) 400 shares (@ ₹110)
Key Takeaway & Spillover Rules:

• Price Priority: Allocates volume strictly from highest bid price down until shares are exhausted. Bids at lower prices receive zero allotment if supply runs out.

• Proportionate: All bids at or above cut-off are pooled together and allotted pro-rata: Allotment = Bid Qty × (Total Available / Aggregate Demand).

• Spillover Mechanics: Undersubscribed non-retail shares roll forward to retail on T+1 Day. Unsubscribed retail shares spill back to carried-forward non-retail bids.

Exchange Operational Matrix & Risk Parameters

Compare clearing constraints, margin funding restrictions, order modification protocols, and operational differences between BSE and NSE OFS bidding segments.

Margin Funding
100% Upfront Required

MTF, intraday leverage, and broker credit funding are strictly prohibited. Institutional investors receive 0% upfront margin subject to custodian confirmation.

Order Modification
Cancel & Re-entry Only

In-place price/volume modifications are disabled on exchange books. Bidders must cancel active orders and submit new order tickets before cutoff.

Broker Tariffs
₹20 + 18% GST Fee

Non-refundable administrative fee assessed per order ticket placed. Applies regardless of whether the bid is allotted, outbid, or rejected by RTA.
